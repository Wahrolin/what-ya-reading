What Ya Reading: standalone version
====================================

Files in this folder:
  index.html              the app
  manifest.webmanifest    app name and icon for your home screen
  sw.js                   lets the app open without internet
  icon-*.png              app icons

The app has no login. Your books are stored in the browser on your phone.
Use Settings > "Save backup file" now and then, and keep the file somewhere
safe (for example Google Drive). "Restore from a backup file" brings it back.

To use it on your phone, the folder must be put online at an https address once
(for example with GitHub Pages or Netlify, both free). Then open that address in
Chrome on your phone and choose "Install app" / "Add to home screen".
